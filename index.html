A problem that hides in plain sight

The problem

Wastage nobody sees

A dripping tap or a forgotten running tap wastes very little water in a minute — but left unnoticed for hours, days, or weeks, that small trickle adds up to thousands of liters. In shared spaces like hostels and campuses, no one owns the tap, so no one notices it's running.

Our solution

AquaGuard

A flow sensor on the tap line feeds live readings to an ESP32, which tells normal use apart from a leak or a tap left running. The moment something's off, an LED and buzzer sound the alarm on the spot — with an optional valve to shut the water off automatically.

What AquaGuard can do

Detect leaks & running taps

Reads live flow rate and duration to spot patterns a normal tap never shows.

Alert instantly

LED and buzzer trigger on the spot, right where the problem is happening.

Track usage over time

Logs flow and wastage so patterns and totals are visible at a glance.

Auto shut-off (optional)

A solenoid valve can close the line automatically once wastage is confirmed.

How the hardware works

A small set of parts, wired together, is enough to watch a tap and speak up the moment something's wrong.

System diagram

Water flow is measured right at the tap, read by the microcontroller, and turned into an alert.

Circuit diagram

A basic wiring layout: everything connects back to the ESP32.

Components

ESP32

The microcontroller at the center of the system — reads the sensor, runs the detection logic, and controls the outputs.

Flow Sensor

Sits in the water line and measures how fast water is moving through the tap.

LED

A simple visual warning — lights up the moment a leak or running tap is detected.

Buzzer

Sounds an audible alert so wastage gets noticed even from across the room.

Solenoid Valve (optional)

Shuts the water off automatically once wastage is confirmed, without anyone needing to step in.

About the project

Project

AquaGuard

AquaGuard is a smart water wastage detection and prevention system. A flow sensor and an ESP32 microcontroller watch a tap in real time, tell ordinary use apart from a leak or a tap left running, and raise an alert — with an optional valve to shut the water off automatically.
